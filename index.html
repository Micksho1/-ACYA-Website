Who we are

Raised in fellowship, sent out in service

The African Church Youth Association (ACYA) is the youth arm of The African Church — "Heaven's Light Our Guide" — established on 13th October 1901, bringing together young members from Parishes, Dioceses, and Provinces across the country for worship, discipleship and shared purpose.

Every year, ACYA gathers its Dioceses and Provinces around three gatherings that shape our calendar: the National Convention, the National Executive Council Meeting, and the National Conference — each one a chance to pray, plan and grow together as one body, "For Christ."

NC

National Convention

Held annually and hosted by a different Diocese or Province each year — our largest gathering of worship, teaching and fellowship.

EC

National Executive Council Meeting

Where ACYA's national leadership meets to review the work of the Association and plan the year ahead.

NC

National Conference

A bi-annual gathering focused on deeper teaching, leadership training and strategy for the Association.
